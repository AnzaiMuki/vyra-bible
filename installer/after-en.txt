VYRA Bible is installed.

1. Start OBS Studio.
2. "Docks" menu: tick "VYRA Bible".
3. In the dock, click "Add the source to OBS": the source that shows the verses is created for you.
4. Type a passage (for example "Jn 3:16"), Enter to preview, Ctrl+Enter to put it on the air.

Global hotkeys: OBS > Settings > Hotkeys, lines "VYRA Bible: ..." (no key is assigned by default).

Your favorites and history are kept when you update or uninstall the plugin.
